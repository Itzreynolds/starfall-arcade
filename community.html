DISCORD STATUS

Ready to connect.

The website already has one central Discord setting. When a permanent invite exists, adding it to site-config.js updates every Discord button across the site.

COMMUNITY STANDARD

Useful feedback over noise.

Feedback should help explain what works, what does not, and why. Testing spaces can keep bug reports separate from general suggestions so development stays organized.
