This starter policy must be reviewed and customized before collecting real user data.

What Starfall is designed to collect

Account identifiers such as email address, display name and handle; profile preferences; game progress and cloud-save data; achievements and activity events; inbox delivery status; purchase, entitlement and subscription records; moderation records; and technical timestamps needed to operate the service.

Payments

Payment card information is intended to be processed by Stripe. Starfall stores payment status and entitlement records, not full card numbers.

Email

Optional promotional email should only be sent where the player has opted in. Account and security messages may be handled separately from marketing preferences.

Your choices

Players should be able to update profile information and marketing preferences and request account/data deletion through a future support workflow.
