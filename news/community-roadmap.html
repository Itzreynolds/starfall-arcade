STARFALL ARCADE UPDATE

Community systems are the next frontier

Coming NextStarfall ArcadeRoadmap

The Starfall Arcade foundation is ready for a proper community layer, but the next systems should be connected only when they are real.

Discord first

A permanent Discord invite can power discussion, bug reports, suggestions, testing, announcements, and events from one organized home.

Accounts after infrastructure

Real Starfall accounts, cloud sync, verified achievements, and live leaderboards require backend services and security work. Those features remain on the roadmap rather than being simulated with fake data.

More games when they are ready

The next game will get a proper reveal instead of filling the library with made-up release details.
