Community systems are next

Coming NextStarfall ArcadeCommunity

The website has space for a community before that community is formally connected. The next step is turning those placeholders into useful player workflows.

What Discord can handle

Separate channels can support announcements, game discussion, bug reports, suggestions, testing, screenshots, and events. Keeping these categories distinct makes feedback easier to use.

Testing future games

As more Starfall Arcade projects appear, selected systems or builds can be tested before public release. The goal is not to promise constant early access, but to use player testing when it actually improves a release.

A permanent Discord invite can be added once and automatically applied to Discord buttons throughout the website.
