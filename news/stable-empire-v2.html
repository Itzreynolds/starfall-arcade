Stable Empire V2 is the flagship release

August 2026Stable EmpireV2

Stable Empire is the first playable game featured through Starfall Arcade, and V2 represents the current expanded release.

What V2 brings together

The release combines deeper horse systems, genetics and breeding, estate progression, a tiered workshop and crafting system, competitions, contracts, story content, cinematics, rival stables, auctions, customer inquiries, weather, and a frontier-style county map.

Long-term progression

Higher stable and facility tiers increasingly depend on crafted components and developed workshop capability, giving the endgame more structure than simply accumulating cash.

Play without moving your save

Stable Empire remains a separate live project. Starfall Arcade links to it rather than replacing it, so the game can preserve its existing browser save system and continue evolving independently.
