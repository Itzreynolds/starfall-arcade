Welcome to Starfall Arcade

August 2026Starfall ArcadeLaunch

Starfall Arcade now has a permanent home. The goal is simple: give every browser game one recognizable place to live without forcing the games themselves into one giant codebase.

What the hub adds

The website includes a game library, individual release pages, development news, a public roadmap, community planning, and a supporter section that can remain inactive until there is a reason to use it.

Why this structure matters

A second or third game should not require rebuilding the entire site. The current library and update feeds are driven by shared configuration data, while individual games can still have their own dedicated pages and repositories.

The first featured release is Stable Empire V2.
