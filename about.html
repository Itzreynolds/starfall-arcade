ABOUT STARFALL ARCADE

Small games. Big worlds.

Starfall Arcade is an independent browser-game hub built around original management, simulation, story, and experimental projects.

THE IDEA

One identity for every game.

Each project can remain technically independent while the arcade gives players one place to discover it, understand it, read updates, follow development, and find the community around it.

THE DIRECTION

Accessible first.

  • Browser-first games that are easy to launch
  • Systems with enough depth to reward returning players
  • Original identities instead of cloning existing game brands
  • Community testing when it improves development
  • Expansion at a pace that keeps projects maintainable
THE NAME

Starfall Arcade

The brand is intentionally broad enough to hold western management games, simulations, stories, minigames, and future experiments without forcing every project into the same visual theme.
