SUPPORT STARFALL ARCADE

Support should add more — not lock away the fun.

Version 1 does not activate payments. The structure is here for a future supporter program that can fund development without turning core gameplay pay-to-win.

FUTURE SUPPORTER PROGRAM

Nothing is being sold yet.

Potential benefits can focus on community recognition, cosmetics, development previews, supporter badges, optional ad-free browsing, and beta opportunities.

STATUSPLANNED FOR LATERNo payment integration is active in Version 1.

Good supporter perks

  • Discord supporter role
  • Profile cosmetics and badges
  • Development previews
  • Optional beta access
  • Ad-free website option if ads ever exist
  • Community recognition

Lines we should not cross

  • No pay-to-win power
  • No fake scarcity pressure
  • No hiding core fixes behind payment
  • Clear pricing and benefits
  • Commercial-use licensing for paid assets
  • Player trust before monetization
