Leaderboards

Live rankings are planned, but they need a real backend and verified game reporting. Version 1 shows the structure without inventing fake players or statistics.

PLANNED

Stable Empire Rankings

Potential categories include competition wins, reputation, stable value, breeding milestones, and seasonal accomplishments — only after verified reporting exists.

PLANNED

Seasonal Boards

Time-limited seasons could reset rankings while preserving permanent profile trophies and historical placements.

PLANNED

Cross-Game Arcade Score

A future account could combine verified achievements from multiple Starfall Arcade games into one player score.

WHY NOT LIVE YET?

Rankings have to be trustworthy.

A static GitHub Pages site cannot securely validate game statistics. Real leaderboards require a server, authentication, rate limiting, anti-tamper checks, and a safe reporting path from each game.

WHEN IT ARRIVES

Build it properly.

  • Verified player accounts
  • Opt-in statistics reporting
  • Season resets and archives
  • Anti-cheat / sanity validation
  • Privacy controls
  • Game-specific ranking categories
